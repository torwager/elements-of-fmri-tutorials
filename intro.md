# Elements of fMRI Analysis — Interactive Tutorials

Welcome! This site provides **hands-on, runnable tutorials** accompanying the book *Elements of Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging* by **Tor D. Wager** and **Martin A. Lindquist**.

The book is a guide for practitioners of fMRI across fields — covering the principles underlying acquisition and analysis of fMRI data, common errors and pitfalls, and emerging techniques. These tutorials turn those principles into practice: every one of the book's **42 chapters** has a companion page here, with concept summaries, worked examples in **MATLAB** (using [CANlab tools](https://canlab.github.io)) and **Python** (using [nilearn](https://nilearn.github.io) and friends), **thought questions** for discussion, and **quiz questions** to check your understanding.

## How the tutorials are organized

The tutorials follow the book's seven parts — see the [full table of contents](./contents.md):

1. **Motivation** — why fMRI, what it can and cannot do, imaging in society
2. **Brain Mapping** — inference about mind and brain, forward/reverse inference, valid and invalid conclusions
3. **MRI Environment and MRI Signal** — physics, BOLD physiology, resolution
4. **Signal Processing and Analysis** — preprocessing, the GLM, group analysis, multiple comparisons, meta-analysis
5. **Experimental Design** — causality, task design, power and sample size
6. **Brain Connectivity** — functional connectivity, PCA/ICA, networks, dynamic and causal models
7. **Predictive Modeling** — machine learning, training and testing models, biomarkers, AI

Start with [How to use this site](./how-to-use.md) and the [software setup guide](./setup.md), or jump straight to any chapter in the sidebar.
